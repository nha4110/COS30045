Rates are not headcounts.

How to read this chart

Choropleth map: country areas are the marks; color bins encode the rate. It makes geographic patterns easy to spot, but exact values are harder to compare than on a shared bar scale. Hover or keyboard-focus a highlighted country for its value.

Design:

Ranked bar chart: country labels identify categories; bar length encodes rate. Hover or focus for a value. Best for comparing exact differences.

Why add bars to a map?

Map color is good for location and broad patterns. Aligned bar lengths are better for ranking and judging the size of differences.
