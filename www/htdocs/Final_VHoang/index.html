Australian road trauma · 2011—2021

A crash is a moment.
The impact lasts.

Every year across Australia, tens of thousands of lives are disrupted in a fraction of a second. Behind every data point lies a person—a driver in traffic, a cyclist at an intersection, a regional worker on a remote highway. This web application transforms complex national health records into a clear, interactive visual story. Stop scrolling and start seeing who is at risk, where crashes happen, and why safety interventions matter.

Explore the data ↓
01 / CONTEXT

These are hospitalisation records, not a count of every crash. Each record represents a person whose road injury required hospital care.

Read trends alongside the reporting notes. Changes in administrative criteria can affect recorded counts.

One dataset. Many human stories.

Read with care

Behind every point is a person.

These hospitalisation data help make patterns visible, but they do not capture every crash, every injury, or the circumstances behind each record. Interpret comparisons with population, exposure, reporting practices and community context in mind.
