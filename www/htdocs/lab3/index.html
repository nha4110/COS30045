Australian Television Energy Consumption Story

Helping Australian households make energy-smart television purchasing choices.

Executive Summary

With rising electricity prices across Australian states, consumer home entertainment electronics contribute heavily to quarterly power bills. This project presents an empirical data story analyzing the energy efficiency, screen dimensions, brand market distribution, and display panel technologies across televisions available in the Australian market.

By exploring government Energy Rating label datasets through KNIME analytics and web visualisations, Australian buyers can evaluate the long-term running costs before committing to a purchase.
