About the data, suppression and interpretation

Source and scope: BITRE Hospitalised Injury, September 2023, based on AIHW data, 2011–2021. The KNIME outputs retain road-traffic cases and use hospitalisation counts excluding deaths under the source definition. Records describe cases, not necessarily unique people.

How the comparisons work: Changes compare 2011 with the selected year. Percentage change is (comparison − baseline) / baseline × 100. The contribution card divides the focused group's count change by the national net count change; it is not a share of all cases. The indexed chart divides each annual count by its own 2011 count × 100. No index or percentage is shown when its denominator is zero or unavailable.

Counts and exposure: These are recorded counts, not rates per population, trip or kilometre. Changes may reflect population, travel exposure, reporting and other factors. They do not establish a change in crash risk or its cause.

Reporting breaks: Victoria's series is not directly comparable with previous years from 2012; New South Wales' from June 2017. The national overview labels these jurisdiction-specific breaks. They must be considered when interpreting long-term changes.

Suppressed and absent records: Suppressed annual totals are blank in the prepared CSVs if an input period was not published. They remain unknown, never zero. First Nations lines contain gaps at suppressed years; no changing mixture of published categories is plotted as a total. A combination absent from a CSV is not automatically assigned zero.

Grouping and geography: Car driver, passenger and unknown-position records are combined as Car occupant; heavy-transport positions are combined similarly to align source categories. State data describe jurisdiction of usual residence. Australia totals also include residence unrecorded or migratory/offshore, so eight jurisdiction totals need not equal National.

Age and sex: The source variable is sex. The labels Missing and intersex or indeterminate or missing are retained as supplied; the latter is not a separate estimate of intersex people. A third-category total is retained in the Step 2 sex-ratio badge when present. Step 2 compares all recorded sex categories; labels are retained as supplied.

First Nations: The supplied CSV includes First Nations people only. It cannot support a First Nations versus Non-Indigenous rate comparison. Interpretation should respect Indigenous data governance and the limitations of source identification.

Sources: BITRE hospitalised injury files and data dictionary. Prepared data and KNIME processing are described in the accompanying README.
