Exercise 4.2 / D3 selections

D3 reaches into the page.

Selections let D3 change existing HTML and build new elements in the document. Here, one small energy-consumption page shows three moves: style a heading, append a paragraph, and draw a rectangle in SVG.

01 — Change and append HTML

A message for the household

This paragraph was already part of the HTML document.

1. Select and style

The heading is selected and its text color is changed with D3.

d3.select("h1")
  .style("color", "#3e7156");

2. Append a paragraph

A new paragraph is added at the end of the existing container.

d3.select("#energy-note")
  .append("p")
        .text("Purchasing a low energy consumption TV will help with your energy bills!");

3. Append an SVG rect

SVG attributes define its position and size; a style makes it visible.

d3.select("#energy-visual")
  .append("rect")
        .attr("x", 50).attr("y", 50)
        .attr("width", 100).attr("height", 30)
        .style("fill", "#3e7156");
